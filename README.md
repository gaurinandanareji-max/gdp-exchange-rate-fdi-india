Impact of GDP and Exchange Rate on FDI: Evidence from India

Project Overview

This project studies the relationship between GDP, exchange rates, and Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) in India.

Objectives

- To examine the relationship between GDP and FDI.
- To analyze the impact of exchange rate movements on FDI.
- To study the relationship between macroeconomic variables and foreign investment.
